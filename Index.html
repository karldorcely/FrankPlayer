Frank Ocean

How his genius changed R&B forever

Frank Ocean born Christopher Edwin Breaux, is an American singer known for his dynamic vocal style and conceptual albums.
He first gained recognition after his critically acclaimed mixtape Nostalgia Ultra, featuring the hit single "Novacane".
His debut Album "Channel Orange" wa released in 2012, earning him a Grammy Award for Best Urban Contemporary Album.
The album featured vintage drums, and lush synthesizers, and explored themes of unrequited love and social issues with songs like Bad Religion and Super Rich Kids.
In 2016, Ocean released his highly anticipated second album "Blonde", which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 chart.
This project blended elements of R&B, pop, electric, hip-hop and avant-garde music, showcasing his versatility as an artist. After Blonde, the music realm changed forever,
with artist using Frank as an inspiration for their own works, and pushing boundaries of R&B music.
